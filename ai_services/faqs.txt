COMPANY FAQ & POLICIES

--- 1. RETURNS & EXCHANGES ---
Return Window: We accept returns and exchanges within 30 days of the delivery date. 
Condition of Items: To be eligible for a return, the clothing or fabric must be unwashed, unworn, and have all original tags attached.
Return Shipping Fee: A standard return shipping fee of $5.99 is deducted from your refund. Exchanges are completely free of charge.
Refund Processing Time: Once we receive the returned item at our warehouse, refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

--- 2. DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS ---
Policy: We take quality control seriously. If you receive a torn, stained, or defective item, you are entitled to a free replacement or a full refund.
How to Report: You must upload a clear photo of the defect in this chat. 
Resolution: Our automated system will verify the damage. If verified, we will instantly issue a replacement or refund. You do not need to ship the defective item back to us; you may discard or donate it.

--- 3. SHIPPING & DELIVERY ---
Standard Shipping: Takes 5-7 business days and costs $4.99. 
Free Shipping: All orders over $75 automatically qualify for free Standard Shipping.
Expedited Shipping: Takes 2-3 business days and costs $12.99.
Order Tracking: Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking link. You can also type "Where is my order?" in this chat to check the status.

--- 4. ORDERS & CANCELLATIONS ---
Order Modifications: We process orders very quickly. You have a 2-hour window after placing an order to cancel it or change the shipping address. After 2 hours, the order is locked and cannot be modified.
Payment Methods: We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay.

--- 5. FABRIC CARE & SIZING ---
Shrinkage: Most of our cotton garments are pre-shrunk, but we recommend washing in cold water and tumble drying on low to prevent any minor shrinkage.
Sizing Guide: Our clothing runs true to size. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a relaxed fit.

--- 6. CUSTOMER SUPPORT ---
Human Support: If the AI assistant cannot resolve your issue, you can ask to speak to a human agent at any time.
Operating Hours: Our live human support agents are available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST. Tickets submitted outside these hours will be answered the next business day.
