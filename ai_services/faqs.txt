COMPANY FAQ & POLICIES:
1. Return Policy: We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn and have original tags.
2. Defective Items: If an item is torn or stained, the user must upload a photo. If approved, we will send a free replacement within 3-5 business days. No need to return the damaged item.
3. Shipping Time: Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Expedited takes 2 days.
4. Support Hours: Human agents are available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM EST.
